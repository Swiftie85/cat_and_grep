


Have you seen the maiden on the the cliff,
Clothed in white above the waves,
When, raging in the stormy mist,
The sea played with the shores,
When a flash of lightning illuminated
Her with a crimson glow at every moment,
And the wind whipped and flew
With her flowing veil?
The sea is beautiful in the stormy mist,
And the sky in its glimmers without azure;
But believe me: the maiden on the cliff
Is more beautiful than the waves, the heavens, and the storm.


(A.S. Pushkin. Poem. 1825)
